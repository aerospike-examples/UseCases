#AdTech Primer

1. Ad serving: The process of delivering advertisements to websites and
applications. This can include display ads, video ads, native ads, and
more.
2. Demand-side platforms (DSPs): Software platforms used by advertisers
and agencies to manage their programmatic advertising buys across multiple
ad exchanges.
3. Supply-side platforms (SSPs): Software platforms that allow publishers
to sell their ad inventory programmatically on various ad exchanges.
4. Ad exchanges: Marketplaces where buyers and sellers of online
advertising inventory transact in real-time.
5. Real-time bidding (RTB): The automated process of buying and selling
online advertising through an auction that takes place in real time, using
data to determine the value of each impression.
6. Header bidding: A technology used by publishers to allow multiple ad
exchanges to compete for their ad inventory at the same time, increasing
competition and potentially yielding higher revenue.
7. Data management platforms (DMPs): Software solutions that allow
advertisers and agencies to collect, organize, and analyze large amounts
of data about their audience in order to target ads more effectively.
8. Ad verification and fraud prevention: Techniques used to ensure that
ads are being served to the intended audience and that ad fraud is
minimized.
9. Mobile ad monetization: Strategies for maximizing revenue from mobile
apps and websites through advertising.
10. Video ads: Types of online advertising that use video content, such as
pre-roll, mid-roll, and post-roll ads, as well as in-stream and out-stream
videos.
11. Native ads: Advertisements that are designed to blend seamlessly with
the content of a website or app, providing a more immersive and less
disruptive user experience.
12. Programmatic TV: The use of automation to buy and sell television
advertising inventory programmatically, allowing for greater targeting and
efficiency compared to traditional methods.
